An owner asks how much a customer owes. Someone checks a register, calls the
accountant and sends a number over WhatsApp. The next question, whether a recent
payment is included, starts the process again.

PumpOS brings the entries behind that answer into a record the owner can inspect.
Customer credit, collections and the outstanding balance belong together.

## The Shift still matters

Fuel Sales come from opening and closing Nozzle Readings. The Shift gives cash
accountability a defined window. When the team counts the Drawer, they should
see the difference between expected and actual cash rather than force the totals
to agree.

A bank collection reduces a customer's dues, but it never enters the Drawer.
Keeping those distinctions clear makes the resulting reports useful.

## A report with a point in time

The Shift Summary records a closed Shift. The Daily Station Sales Report, or
DSSR, brings together the Business Day's closed-shift summaries and financial
records as of generation. Later financial entries do not silently rewrite that
saved report.

That history matters when a question comes up days or months later.

## Starting with a guided pilot

We're starting with individual stations and hands-on onboarding. The pilot is
a chance to work through real workflows, understand where teams need help and
improve the product from that experience.
