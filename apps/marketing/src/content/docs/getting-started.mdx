This guide outlines the setup for your first station. Pilot access and onboarding
are arranged with the PumpOS team. [Book a demo](/demo) if you do not have access yet.

## 1. Create your organization

Sign in and complete the onboarding wizard. You'll set your organization name,
legal details, and the first station's basics, including timezone and Business
Day start time. A fuel day commonly runs from 06:00 to 06:00 the following morning.

## 2. Model your forecourt

Add your **tanks**, **dispensers**, **nozzles**, and **products** with prices.
Nozzles map to tanks and products, which is how PumpOS derives fuel sales from
Nozzle Readings.

## 3. Open your first shift

From **Shifts**, open a Shift and capture opening Nozzle Readings and the opening
cash float. Check the Business Date before saving.

Continue with [Running shifts](/docs/running-shifts) for daily operation and close.
