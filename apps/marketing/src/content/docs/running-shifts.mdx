---
title: Running shifts
description: Open, operate, and close a shift with accurate reconciliation.
order: 1
category: Operations
---

A Shift is an attendant-accountability window for the Drawer. Every operational
record also belongs to a Business Day.

## Opening

Capture opening nozzle readings (they default from the previous close). Record
the opening cash float.

## During the shift

- **Fuel Sales** are derived from Nozzle Readings rather than manual sales entries.
- Record **expenses**, **collections**, **purchases**, and **credit sales** as
  they happen. Only cash movements touch the drawer.

## Closing

Enter closing readings and count the drawer. PumpOS reconciles:

```
expectedDrawerCash =
  openingCash + cashSales + cashCollections
  − drawerExpenses − drawerSupplierPayments − cashDrops
```

Any difference is recorded as **variance**, so it remains available for review.
Closing creates a **Shift Summary** snapshot. Where reopening is permitted, it
is an explicit action. Closing the Business Day locks its Shifts.

## The daily report

The DSSR brings together closed-shift summaries and Business Day financials as
of generation. Financial entries recorded later do not change the saved snapshot.

## Credit and non-cash collections

A Credit Sale increases a customer receivable. A Collection reduces it.
Bank, card and UPI Collections do not affect Drawer cash. You can inspect these
entries in the customer statement without including them in cash reconciliation.
