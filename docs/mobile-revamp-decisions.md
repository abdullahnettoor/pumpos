# Mobile revamp: product decisions

The decisions taken while building the mobile revamp (#388), with the current
default and where to change it. Architectural decisions are ADRs:
[0007](adr/0007-snapshots-grow-additively.md) (snapshots grow additively),
[0008](adr/0008-mobile-access-by-role.md) (mobile access by Role) and
[0009](adr/0009-long-lived-branches-carry-no-migrations.md) (no migrations on
long-lived branches). Change a default here and in the code together.

| Decision                                                                   | Current default                                                                                                                                                                           | Why                                                                                                         | Where to change                                                                                                                                         | Size of change                                                              |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Stock variance tolerance** (Insights → Stock loss badge, DSSR tank rows) | 0.5% of litres sold, one app-wide value. **Provisional: awaiting an owner decision.**                                                                                                     | Temperature and meter rounding make small gaps normal; 0.5% flags only gaps worth checking.                 | `STOCK_VARIANCE_TOLERANCE_PCT`, `packages/shared/src/utils/stock-variance.ts`                                                                           | One constant. A per-product or per-station value needs a setting.           |
| **Days of cover** (Home tank gauges)                                       | Book stock ÷ average daily sales **per tank** over the last 7 closed Business Days (fewer for a new station). Only metered (Nozzle Reading) sales count. Hidden for a tank with no sales. | Two tanks of the same product can run out on different days; a per-product average hides which one.         | `DAYS_OF_COVER_WINDOW`, `packages/shared/src/utils/days-of-cover.ts`; `packages/core/src/capabilities/inventory/tank-days-of-cover.ts`                  | Window: one constant. Per product: the core use-case and its tests.         |
| **Week tile** (Reports)                                                    | "Last 7 days": the 7 completed Business Days before today, compared with the 7 before those, counting only days sealed in both. Not a calendar week.                                      | Like-for-like on every weekday; a calendar week compares a 2-day Tuesday with a full previous week.         | `weekWindows`, `packages/core/src/capabilities/reporting/business-day-list/list-business-days.ts`; label in `apps/mobile/src/screens/ReportsScreen.tsx` | Small: one pure function, the label and their tests. No API or data change. |
| **"vs previous period"** (Insights)                                        | Average per closed day, shown only when both periods have at least 3 closed days.                                                                                                         | Avoids "▲250%" when one period has 2 closed days and the other 7.                                           | `INSIGHTS_MIN_COMPARABLE_DAYS`, `packages/shared/src/types/insights.ts`                                                                                 | One constant.                                                               |
| **Supplier category**                                                      | None. Supplier rows show name, trade name or phone, N unpaid, since <date>.                                                                                                               | Suppliers have no category in the data and nothing needs one; the Supplier page lists purchases by product. | —                                                                                                                                                       | Would need a schema field plus the desktop supplier form.                   |
| **Vendor code, tanker number**                                             | Shown only if recorded; nothing records them today.                                                                                                                                       | Not in the data.                                                                                            | `apps/mobile/src/lib/money/parties.ts` (vendor code), supplier statement rows                                                                           | Needs the desktop forms to capture them first.                              |
| **Supplier payment method** (statements)                                   | The Funding Account's type (Bank, Cash in Hand, …).                                                                                                                                       | Supplier Payments store no method of their own.                                                             | `packages/ui/src/services/reports/partyStatement.ts`                                                                                                    | Small.                                                                      |
| **Mobile Collections: Card/UPI terminals**                                 | Not offered. A Card or UPI Collection posts to a Funding Account the user picks.                                                                                                          | Keeps the mobile sheet short; terminals stay a desktop flow.                                                | `apps/mobile/src/lib/money/collection.ts`                                                                                                               | Medium: add the terminal picker and its tests.                              |
| **Product categories on old reports**                                      | Snapshots saved before categories existed show a single "Products" line.                                                                                                                  | Snapshots are never backfilled ([ADR 0007](adr/0007-snapshots-grow-additively.md)).                         | `apps/mobile/src/lib/home/byProduct.ts`                                                                                                                 | Small.                                                                      |
| **Closing a Business Day**                                                 | Desktop only, for every Role.                                                                                                                                                             | Closing seals the DSSR; it belongs at the desk where the counts are done.                                   | —                                                                                                                                                       | Out of scope for mobile.                                                    |
| **Dark mode**                                                              | Built but hidden; the app is always Light. A dev-only switch (`vite dev` or `VITE_THEME_DEV_SWITCH`) shows Dark.                                                                          | Dark mode rolls out across desktop, console and mobile together.                                            | `APPEARANCE_ENABLED`, `apps/mobile/src/theme/config.ts`                                                                                                 | One flag, once the other apps are ready.                                    |
| **Handover strip wording**                                                 | Variance reads like the rest of the app ("−₹125", "Balanced"), using the shared tolerance.                                                                                                | One rule and one wording everywhere.                                                                        | `apps/mobile/src/lib/variance.ts`                                                                                                                       | Small.                                                                      |

Open follow-ups found during the revamp: #419 (product cash counted per DU in
the server Handover), #442 (flaky PDF test), #443 (server allows the last Owner
to be locked out).
