Every attendant now has their own drawer: give each one an opening float when you open the shift, and every dispenser in use needs someone on it, even if a manager or the owner covers it.
When an attendant hands over, their shortage or excess is shown on its own, and shift close now shows it apart from the office's own cash count; closing waits until every drawer has been handed over.
Collections, expenses, supplier payments and bank deposits now go on the calendar date you enter them and appear in the new Daily Cash Book, while the daily sales report covers sales only.
The new Attendant Handover Report shows each attendant's sales, credit and cash day by day, and Print now gives you exactly the same PDF as Save PDF.
