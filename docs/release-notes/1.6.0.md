Reports now have their own Templates tab: pick paper size, choose which sections print on the Shift Summary, Daily DSSR and Attendant Handover Report, reorder them and preview the page before you save. Owners and Managers can change templates.
On the Handover drawer, cash drops now sit on their own "+ Add cash drop" row, so adding one no longer pushes Credit Chits down.
The desktop app checks for updates when it starts, even before you sign in, and stays quiet unless one is waiting. Click the version in the status bar to check by hand. Windows and Linux now use the normal title bar, and the first window opens larger.
Nothing changes in how you open or close a shift.
