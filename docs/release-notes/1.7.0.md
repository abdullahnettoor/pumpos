Tanker unloading now has its own Decantation screen: record each chamber into its tank and the dip, and that is what adds fuel to tank stock. A fuel purchase bill is now an office entry that no longer changes stock, and density readings have their own register.
Fuel prices can be scheduled ahead, and Open shift shows the rate each fuel will sell at for the whole shift. A shift will not open while a fuel on a working pump has no rate.
An attendant on two dispensers now has their oil and product cash counted in one drawer only, so the other drawer no longer reads short. A product with no MRP cannot go on a handover until one is set.
Every screen has a cleaner look, the phone app has a new Home, DSSR is now called DSR, saves show progress straight away and a refresh keeps you on the same page.
Your plan now sets how many people can sign in: 3 on Core, 10 on Pro and unlimited on Pro+. Attendants handing over on their own phone need Pro or Pro+. Closing a shift works as before.
