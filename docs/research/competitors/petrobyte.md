## 2. Their USPs

1. **Price.** "Starts from ₹300/month". The decennial plan works out to ₹3,600/yr, and they promote it as "the cost of a cup of tea". This is their main pitch.
2. **Full financial accounting built in:** Balance Sheet, P&L, Cash Flow, Day Book, Receipts & Payments, Trial Balance, and 6 voucher types. They call themselves "petrol pump *accounting* software". **This is the same positioning as ours.**
3. **Tally integration:** export of masters and vouchers for import into TallyPrime.
4. **GST returns in one click:** GSTR-1, GSTR-3B summary, outward supplies (B2B, B2C, non-GST, HSN), GST transaction summaries.
5. **Shift workflow:** each shift goes through Evaluate → Close → Settle → Verify.
6. **Automatic payroll:** gross pay, allowances, absences, overtime, expenses and commission.
7. **Credit customer billing:** credit sale cycle, invoices per vehicle, online indent (fuel order), customer app, SMS, email and WhatsApp.
8. **Short-term credit** (local or per-entry credit) and **cash counter billing** (PDF or print bill with SMS).
9. **POS settlement:** a separate ledger account for each POS terminal, so receipts and settlements can be matched.
10. **Tank lorry and mobile bowser** management, each with its own P&L; **stock inspection** for loss from tampering, evaporation and short receipts.
11. **8+ dashboards** (business, petroleum, customers, profits) and **40+ reports** exportable to PDF, Excel and CSV.
12. **"Your data, no lock-in":** data can be exported or moved to Tally or Excel at any time, including after the plan expires.
13. **5 user types with unlimited logins.**

## 6. Pricing implications (revises the FuelSetu report, §6)

> Superseded by the current recommendation in [README §2.4](./README.md#24-recommended-pumpos-pricing), which adds monthly prices. Kept for the reasoning.

The market spread for one pump for one year, excl. GST:

| Vendor | Model | Effective ₹/yr |
|---|---|---|
| PetroByte | All features; by term | **₹3,600 – ₹7,700** |
| FuelSetu | All features; by term | ₹12,600 – ₹18,000 |
| Petrosoft | 4 tiers, gated | ₹17,000 – ₹48,000 |

PetroByte sets the floor of the market at about ₹600 a month. We should **not** compete with it on price alone:
- At ₹3–7k a year there is little room for onboarding support or WhatsApp message costs.
- A race to the bottom undermines the "correctness and accountability" story.
- Dealers who buy on price alone tend to switch often.

### Recommended stance

Position PumpOS in the **middle of the market**, priced at or below FuelSetu and well below Petrosoft, and justify the gap over PetroByte with leakage prevention. The sales line: *"We find ₹X lakh a year in shortages and stock loss. The software costs ₹1,000 a month."*

| | **Basic** | **Pro** (anchor) | **Business** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Per station per year (excl. GST) | **₹6,999** | **₹12,999** | **₹19,999** for the first station, ₹9,999 for each extra |
| Monthly equivalent | ~₹583 (matches PetroByte's annual) | ~₹1,083 | ~₹1,667 |
| 3-year term | 25% off | 25% off | Custom |
| Who it's for | Price-sensitive single pump | Pump with attendants and credit customers | Groups and high-volume pumps |

What each tier contains:
- **Basic** matches PetroByte on core features and price:
  - shifts, handover, DSSR, stock, credit, GST/VAT
  - ledger and financial statements
  - Tally and Excel export, GSTR-1/3B
- **Pro adds:**
  - attendant and manager app seats
  - WhatsApp alerts and reminders
  - credit statements and aging, with enforced credit limits
  - POS settlement matching
  - attendance and payroll
- **Business adds:**
  - consolidated reports across stations
  - interest on late payment, E-Invoicing
  - audit explorer
  - dedicated support

Alternative worth testing: **price by monthly volume** (PetroByte's table A). It scales fairly. Small rural pumps pay less, high-volume pumps get more value and pay more, and dealers find it easy to understand. Example: under 50 KL ₹6,999, 50–150 KL ₹12,999, over 150 KL ₹19,999, all with the same features.

Growth levers to copy:
- 15-day trial with no setup fee.
- Referral credit (one month free per referral).
- Published yearly price increases, to reward early and long commitments.
- Guaranteed data export, even after a plan lapses.
