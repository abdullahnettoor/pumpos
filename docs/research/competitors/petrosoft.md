## 2. Their USPs

1. **Four role apps:** Dealer, Manager, Pump Boy (attendant) and Credit Customer.
2. **Compliance for the CA:** GSTR-1/2A/2B/3B, B2B and B2C reports, monthly VAT and TCS statements, Tally export, and (in higher plans) E-Way Bill and E-Invoicing.
3. **Credit recovery:** period and vehicle-wise statements, a record per indent (fuel order slip), credit limits that block a sale, interest on late payment with grace days, and auto SMS, email or WhatsApp for dues.
4. **Stock depth:** density readings, decantation (ordered, received and unloaded quantity), variance between dip-based sale and meter sale, and Sales Officer inspection reports.
5. **Staff:** attendance, duty-based payroll, hand loans and advances, monthly attendant shortage reports.
6. **Extras:** loyalty, expiry and renewal reminders, thermal POS printing, tanker, bowser and warehouse modules.
7. **Report count as a selling point:** 40, 60 or 90+ reports depending on plan.

### How their tiers are designed

- **Lite (₹17k) is a desktop-only bookkeeping plan.** It has the full operational core (shifts, stock, credit, GST, density, bank) but no attendant, manager or customer apps, no notifications and no payroll.
- **Pro (+₹5k) unlocks people:** mobile apps with seat caps (2 managers, 10 attendants, 10 credit customers), notifications, payroll and tanker sale.
- **Max (+₹10k) is the anchor:** unlimited apps, E-Invoicing and E-Way Bill, interest on credit sales, loyalty, Sales Officer inspection, and the tanker, bowser and warehouse modules. Labelling it "most popular" pushes buyers here.
- **Premium (+₹16k)** is Max plus customisation and custom reports.
- **What drives an upgrade:** mobile app seats, notifications, recovery tools (interest) and compliance (E-Invoicing). Core operations are never gated. Every plan is a complete, usable pump system.

## 6. Pricing and packaging recommendation for PumpOS

> Superseded: these prices were lowered after the FuelSetu, PetroByte and PumpOne reviews. The current recommendation is in [README §2.4](./README.md#24-recommended-pumpos-pricing). Kept for the reasoning.

Today PumpOS has a single `CORE` plan with a 1-station limit, in `packages/core/src/capabilities/organization-access/registry.ts`. The gating machinery (capabilities, limits, restricted access) is already built, so new tiers are mostly registry entries.

### Principles (learned from their tiers)

1. **Never gate the core.** Shifts, nozzle readings, handover, stock, credit, the daily sales report, GST/VAT and the ledger stay in every plan. Shortages pinned to the right attendant is our edge, so every customer must get it.
2. **Upgrade on people, reach and automation:** attendant and manager seats, notifications, exports, multi-station. Not on correctness.
3. **Price below them on entry and match them in the middle.** We are new and unproven, so the entry price is how we get pumps to switch.
4. **Charge per station, per year, with a monthly option.** SaaS that is cheaper to start beats a large upfront fee for a single-pump dealer.

Notes:

- **Density stays in Basic** because Petrosoft includes it in Lite and it's an oil-company compliance need. Gating it would lose deals.
- **Tally and GSTR exports in Pro** are the main reason to upgrade from Basic. They are also the top switching blocker, so Pro has to be priced to be easy to say yes to.
- **Launch offer:** 3 months free, or the first year at the Basic price on Pro, for pumps switching from Petrosoft or paper. Include free data migration (opening readings, credit balances, supplier balances).
- **Mapping to our gating:** each ✗ row becomes a Product Capability key (for example `exports.tally`, `notifications.whatsapp`, `crm.credit_interest`, `reports.cross_station`). Seats and stations become Limits (`station_count`, plus new `attendant_seats` and `manager_seats`). See the `pump-access-gating` skill.

### Open questions to settle before publishing prices

1. Petrosoft's billing period and renewal price. Ask as a prospective buyer.
2. Is their price per station, or per organisation?
3. Our cost per station (Supabase, WhatsApp and SMS per-message cost), to set the floor price.
4. How much will dealers pay monthly vs yearly? Ask in every customer interview.
